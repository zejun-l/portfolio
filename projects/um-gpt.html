Overview

This project centered on redesigning the interface of U-M GPT, the University of Michigan’s custom generative AI platform, including a new feature intended to improve the student experience. Completed during a semester-long introductory UX course, the work moved through the full design process—from the client brief and initial sketches to low- and high-fidelity wireframes—with an emphasis on strong storytelling and thoughtful design choices.
